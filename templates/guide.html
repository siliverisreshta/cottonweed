Parthenium (Gajar Ghas)

• White small flowers • Leaves look like carrot leaves • Strong bad smell when crushed • Causes allergies

How to Control:

  • Metsulfuron-methyl 20 WP: 4g in 10L water
  • Spray when weed is small (15-20 days old)
  • Wear gloves and mask - can cause skin rash

Nutgrass (Motha)

• Purple-brown flower tops • Triangular stems • Deep underground roots (motha) • Very hard to remove

How to Control:

  • Halosulfuron-methyl 75 WG: 70g per acre
  • Spray twice - after 15 days gap
  • Summer deep plowing helps control

Bathua (Chilrani)

• White powder on leaves • Diamond-shaped leaves • Grows quickly after rains • Many seeds in soil

How to Control:

  • Pendimethalin 30 EC: 1L/acre before sowing
  • Glyphosate: 1.2-1.6L/acre after crop
  • Remove by hand before flowering

Johnson Grass (Jangli Jowar)

• Looks like jowar crop • White line in leaf center • Purple flowers • Underground roots spread fast

How to Control:

  • Glyphosate: 2L/acre (spot spray only)
  • Cut repeatedly to weaken roots
  • Deep plow in summer heat

Field Bindweed (Hiranpadi)

• White-pink funnel flowers • Climbs on cotton plants • Arrow-shaped leaves • Spreads through deep roots

How to Control:

  • 2,4-D: 1L/acre when weed is young
  • Pull out roots completely
  • Repeat control needed

Cocklebur (Chhota Gokhru)

• Spiky seeds stick to clothes • Big rough leaves • Harmful for animals • Grows 3-4 feet tall

How to Control:

  • Pendimethalin: 1L/acre before sowing
  • Remove by hand before seed forms
  • Clean farm tools to stop spread
